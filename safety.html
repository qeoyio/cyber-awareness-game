How to Stay Safe on the Internet

1. Read Terms and Conditions

Always review what you are agreeing to. Understand what information a website may collect and how it may use that information.

2. Protect Personal Information

Avoid sharing your full name, email, address, phone number, or other personal details unless necessary.

3. Check Website Addresses

Make sure the website address is correct. Watch out for misspellings and suspicious links.

4. Look for HTTPS

HTTPS encrypts information sent between your browser and the website. However, it does not guarantee that a website is trustworthy.

5. Use Strong Passwords

Create unique passwords for different accounts. Consider using a password manager and multifactor authentication.

6. Avoid Suspicious Downloads

Never download unknown files or click suspicious pop-ups and advertisements.

7. Think Before Clicking

Be careful with links in emails, messages, and unfamiliar websites. Scammers often use fake offers to trick visitors.

Remember!

Your personal information is valuable. Think before you click, share, or accept.
